Verify a person

A one-time identity check, run by ByteDance through Comfy. Once verified, a person can appear in any ad without checking again.

What happens
  • The photo is sent to Comfy, and from there to ByteDance for the check.
  • You get a link. Open it on your phone and do a short liveness check (about 30 seconds). Reelmatch never sees that video.
  • The two ids that come back are saved on this computer only, so you can reuse them.
